Private Dining

A traditional
Japanese room.

A more private, authentic setting in downtown Calgary.

For special occasions and smaller gatherings, Fifth Avenue Sushi offers seating in a traditional Japanese-style room. It isn't a fully enclosed private space, but it's set apart from the main dining area for a more private, intimate feel — the authentic way to enjoy Japanese cuisine.

In keeping with tradition, guests remove their shoes and are seated on the floor at a low table. It's a memorable, hands-on experience for anyone looking to celebrate a special occasion or simply enjoy an evening the traditional way.

Reserve the room

The traditional room is limited, so we recommend booking ahead. Reserve your table through OpenTable, or call us directly to request the traditional room and tell us about your occasion.
