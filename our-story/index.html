Our Story

How Fifth Avenue Sushi
came to be.

Authentic Japanese cuisine in downtown Calgary.

Fifth Avenue Sushi sits on 5th Avenue SE in the heart of downtown Calgary, where we share the flavours and traditions of authentic Japanese cuisine with our neighbours and visitors alike. From hand-pressed nigiri and thick-cut sashimi to ramen, udon and Japanese hot dishes, everything we serve is prepared with respect for the ingredients and the craft behind them — and guests have rated the experience 4.9 stars on Google.

Authentic Japanese cuisine, downtown

Fifth Avenue Sushi was built around a straightforward belief: that real Japanese food deserves a proper home in downtown Calgary. Our kitchen runs two sides. At the sushi bar, nigiri is hand-pressed and sashimi sliced to order, alongside maki and chef's specialty rolls like the 5th Avenue Signature Roll, the Flying Eel Dragon Roll and the Lobster Roll. On the hot side, the kitchen sends out shoyu and miso ramen, udon and yakisoba, teriyaki, kakuni braised pork and gyoza made with AAA Alberta beef. It's a full picture of Japanese dining, not just a single specialty. Explore the full menu →

Craft and freshness

Our kitchen is led by Executive Chef Varamon Prua, who has spent more than 18 years perfecting the art of sushi. His philosophy is simple: nothing beats freshness. Every sushi order is prepared fresh, only after it's placed — nothing is pre-made — so each plate reaches the table at its very best. That commitment to doing things properly is at the core of who we are.

A more traditional experience

Beyond the main dining room, Fifth Avenue Sushi offers seating in a traditional Japanese-style room, where guests remove their shoes and are seated on the floor at a low table — a more private, authentic way to enjoy a meal. It reflects how we think about Japanese dining: the food matters, but so does the whole experience. You can read more on our private dining page.

Rooted in downtown Calgary

You'll find us at 118A 5th Ave SE, with free parking directly behind the restaurant — a rare thing downtown, and worth knowing if you're driving in. We're open seven days a week for lunch and dinner, and beyond the dining room you can enjoy Fifth Avenue Sushi through pickup and DoorDash delivery, or reserve a table on OpenTable. Whether it's a weekday lunch, a family dinner or a special occasion, we're glad to share authentic Japanese cuisine with you.
