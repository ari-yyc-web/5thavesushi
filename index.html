Our kitchen runs two sides. At the sushi bar, nigiri is hand-pressed and sashimi sliced to order — salmon, tuna, smoked salmon, ebi, unagi, toro and tamago — alongside maki and chef's specialty rolls such as the 5th Avenue Signature Roll, the Flying Eel Dragon Roll, the Red Smoked Dragon Roll and the Lobster Roll. On the hot side, the kitchen sends out shoyu and miso ramen, udon and yakisoba stir-fries, teriyaki chicken and salmon, kakuni braised pork, tonkatsu, poke bowls and gyoza made with AAA Alberta beef.

Nigiri starts at $3 to $4 a piece and sashimi at $8 for two pieces. Eight-piece maki rolls run $10 to $18, and the specialty rolls range from $15 to $28. Ramen bowls are $23 to $24 and come with your choice of chicken, beef, pork or tofu. Rice and vegetable plates — teriyaki chicken, teriyaki salmon, lemon garlic snapper, NY steak — are served with rice and mixed vegetables, and you can add chili oil on the side to any dish for a dollar.

Feeding a group? Our party trays come as a 40-piece Platter A or a 44-piece Platter B, available for take-out with advance notice. Bento plates are served at lunch only, and pair sushi and sashimi with a protein stir-fry, miso soup and house salad.

Allergies and dietary needs

Please tell your server about any allergies or dietary restrictions before ordering, and the kitchen will do its best to accommodate you. Our kitchen handles gluten, shellfish, fish, egg, soy, sesame, dairy and sulphites, so gluten-free ingredients are not suitable for guests with celiac disease because of possible cross-contamination. Vegetarian guests are welcome — let us know when you order and we can modify the ramen and a number of other dishes for you.

Groups, delivery and takeout

Beyond the dining room, we host private dining and larger corporate bookings, and our party trays are available for take-out when ordered ahead. For nights in, Fifth Avenue Sushi is available for delivery and pickup through DoorDash in addition to dine-in. For a bigger group or anything more specific, calling the restaurant directly is the fastest way to sort out the details.
