# Fifth Avenue Sushi - menu photos

60 images, 600x800 (3:4 portrait), 4 MB total.
Sized for featured dish cards.

Folders mirror the menu categories. Filenames are slugs of the real
menu item names. images.csv pairs every file with its name, price and
description.

## Contents

- Appetizers: 15
- Bento Box (Lunch Only): 2
- Mains: 7
- Ramen: 1
- Rice: 2
- Sashimi: 6
- Special Rolls: 6
- Stir-Fry: 4
- Sushi (Nigiri): 8
- Sushi Rolls: 9

## Before publishing - please read

1. These photos originate from the previous ordering site, where each
   was labelled "Photo for Reference Only". They are platform stock
   images, not photographs of this restaurant's own food. Confirm you
   have the right to republish them before they go live.

2. Each image was extended by AI from a 600x600 original to reach 3:4.
   Roughly 69% of each final image is generated. On dishes that already
   filled the frame, the model added extra food - so the portion shown
   can be larger than what is actually served. Compare against the
   originals in downloads/ before using any image where portion size
   matters to the customer.

3. 37 menu items have no photo at all (see missing_items.csv).
